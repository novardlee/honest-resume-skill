# Privacy policy: Honest Resume skill

Last updated: November 2, 2026

The Honest Resume skill is a set of instructions and a template that run inside your own AI agent. It has no server, no account, no analytics and no network calls of its own.

- **What it collects:** nothing. The publisher (Novard Lee) receives no data from your use of the skill.
- **Your information:** the facts you share (work history, education, target roles) stay in your conversation with your agent and, if your agent has file access, in a private `profile.json` you control. The skill tells the agent to keep these records private.
- **What it never asks for:** passwords, MFA codes or government ID numbers.
- **Your agent and other tools:** your AI provider, and any job board or email tool you connect, handle data under their own privacy policies.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/novardlee/honest-resume-skill/issues.
